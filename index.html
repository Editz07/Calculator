// About This Calculator
This tool calculates your dollar risk on Futures contracts, or the lot size required on MT5, based on your stop loss distance and risk tolerance. Use the switch above the instrument selector to toggle between Futures and MT5 modes. A red outline on an input means it's blank, invalid, or zero. Built and maintained by editztrades.
